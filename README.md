### 🧭 About Me

I'm a Data Scientist and AI Engineer based in Islamabad, Pakistan, focused on turning large, messy datasets into clear, actionable insight — through dashboards, statistical analysis, and NLP-driven text analytics. I'm currently completing an **MS in Data Science at FAST-NUCES**, researching how to tell genuine video signals apart from synthetically manipulated ones — work that sits at the intersection of data integrity, digital forensics, and responsible AI.

I've built end-to-end ML pipelines, multi-agent LLM systems, and multilingual NLP tools, and I like working independently across distributed, remote teams.

- 🔬 **Currently researching:** Robust video source provenance — distinguishing organic sensor noise from synthetic/adversarial injection attacks
- 🤖 **Currently building:** Multi-agent AI systems and LLM-based agentic pipelines
- 🌱 **Currently exploring:** Embedding-based semantic matching for LLM web/GUI agents
- 🎯 **Looking for:** Remote/home-based roles worldwide, plus onsite opportunities in Pakistan and the wider region
- 💬 **Ask me about:** ML pipelines, NLP, LLM agent orchestration, data storytelling, or digital forensics
